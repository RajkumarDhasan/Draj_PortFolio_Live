Portfolio audit and redesign direction

Audit of Rajkumar Portfolio v2, the version with the full space journey. The goal is two messages within 30 seconds: research-led product designer first, creative explorer second.

1 · What the code shows

Works well · keep
  • Type pairing (Clash Display + Satoshi), near-black ground, single orange accent #FF5A3C. Restrained and distinctive.
  • Hash routes (#/work, #/project/…, #/about) give every case study a direct link.
  • Separate mobile app shell with tabs, swipe and safe-area handling.
  • Real Taggo, TheEvents and Noma screens, wireframe PDF viewer, image slots.
  • Fit check and Ask my work: useful recruiter tools, already wired to project data.
  • A global reduced-motion rule exists, and the intro has a Skip button.
Gets in the way · change
  • The intro gates the site for about 21 seconds (5 planet stages × 1.75s, then the black hole and exit). It plays on every visit. Nothing is remembered between sessions.
  • Skip appears after a 0.8s fade and is small and uppercase. Recruiters may not see it.
  • The intro is a full-screen canvas with 260 stars and textured planets on phones as well as desktop.
  • The hero says "Design is my primary skill. Code and AI are my accelerators." It describes tools, not research.
  • Case studies open with a "Cinematic Intro" block, and a CosmicLesson space-fact popup appears on project pages while visitors read.
  • The custom cursor replaces the native one and can hide focus and link affordance.
  • Five portfolio versions (v1–v5) exist. It is unclear which one is canonical.
Research integrity · claims that need your confirmation

These appear in v2 as facts. The workbook says not to publish them without evidence. I will hide each one until you confirm how it was measured.

2 · The space theme: keep it, but stop it from blocking the work

The theme supports your second message, so removing it would cost you something. The problem is where it sits. Right now it comes before the work. In the redesign it moves to the edges: background, transitions, the About page and the ending. Every element must pass one test: it never delays, covers or competes with reading.

21s planet introRemoved from page load. Kept as an optional 20s "Take the long way" film, started from the footer.
Hero backgroundStatic, sparse starfield with one dim planet horizon at the bottom edge. No glow drift. Slight parallax on desktop only.
Black holeBecomes the contact section's ending visual, a still image or a slow 1-loop motion. It's the end of the map, not a gate.
CosmicLesson popupsRemoved from case studies. The facts move into About under "Why space".
Custom cursorNative cursor everywhere. Visible focus rings.
Reduced motion and mobileFully static under reduced motion. No canvas on phones, only a CSS background.
