About

Design is my primary skill. Code and AI are my accelerators.

UI/UX Designer with 6+ years of experience designing end-to-end digital products across mobility, fintech and e-commerce in the UAE. At Kief Consultancies & Technologies (Abu Dhabi), I've served as the sole designer across multiple live products — owning the full lifecycle: user research, information architecture, high-fidelity UI, design systems and developer-ready Figma handoffs.

I bring a hybrid background in full-stack development (HTML, CSS, Java), which lets me collaborate precisely with engineers, and I apply UX psychology principles — Fitts' Law, Hick's Law, Gestalt, Jakob's Law — to deliver intuitive, research-informed experiences.
