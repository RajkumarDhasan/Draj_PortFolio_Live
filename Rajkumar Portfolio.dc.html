04 · How I research

Every screen traces back to evidence.

  1. 01EvidenceWhat people say and do: interviews, usability sessions, support and analytics data.
  2. 02InsightThe pattern behind the evidence, written as a problem worth solving.
  3. 03DecisionA design change tied to that insight, with the alternatives I set aside.
  4. 04ValidationTesting the decision with users before it ships.
  5. 05OutcomeWhat changed, reported only when it was measured.
